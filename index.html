A little more epic. Still your original message.
The Sirmonator preserves your manuscript and passes its passages to a speech engine, with only the optional literal pronunciation substitutions you review. No chat model or automatic rewrite step. Natural delivery respects sentence and paragraph boundaries and adds silent pauses. Numbers and abbreviations follow the voice engine’s pronunciation rules. Speech synthesis can still mispronounce, omit, or repeat words; perfect spoken accuracy is not guaranteed. Review important passages alongside the text.
